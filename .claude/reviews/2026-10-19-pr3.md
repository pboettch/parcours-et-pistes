# Reviews of PR #3 (runs, dog path, single writer), 2026-10-18/19

Eight `protocol-reviewer` rounds shaped this PR; the history was rewritten afterwards (the earlier
commits are on the local branch `archive/runs-dog-path-pre-rewrite`).

What the reviews led to (user: "for concurrent things, better remove features than complicate"):
- **Leader compaction of the dog path removed** (concurrent compactors lost points; purge, chunk
  ids linking pseudonyms, editor removal destroying chunks): the recorder alone writes its 20
  rolling points and its full track (segments of 1000 points).
- **Editors removed**: one writer per item (`Writers.assigned`, per-track owners); runs owner-only;
  the owner re-signs on reassignment / ownership transfer; restricted items follow.
- **Password change removed** (owner repairs were exploitable; carrying members' own items across
  a key change needed seeds and re-encryption): a compromised project is replaced by a new one.
- **Ownership transfer removed** (5th round: an interrupted transfer made the project
  unjoinable): the owner is the creator, pinned by the join link; ACL `owner` field, no chain,
  no offers; `meta` has no revision and never changes.
- Fixed on the way: stale decode cache; tie rule (same rev and time: last received wins; same
  signed content = same version); revisions above `maxItemRev` rejected; writes serialized;
  resubscribe + sync barrier after reconnects before writing; Mqtt5Transport failing pending
  publishes on disconnect and using a new client id per connect; hashed device tags in topics;
  out-of-range times rejected as content; `close()`/`join()`/`deleteProject()` edge cases;
  updating a hidden track keeps its visibility. 5th round: out-of-range envelope times and any
  unexpected error while handling a message only reject that message; idempotent MQTT subscribe;
  a reconnect replaces a running reload and only the latest reload's failure fails a write; dead
  connections dropped (keep-alive) and broker-side disconnects reconnected; owner re-signs allowed
  above `maxItemRev`; invalid recipient keys rejected; `close`/`deleteChannel` refuse later
  writes and wait for earlier ones; `deviceId` validated before any channel exists; an
  unreadable last full-track segment no longer blocks a device. 6th round: a failed
  `close`/`deleteChannel` can be retried (best-effort teardown, `_closing` reset); concurrent
  `connect()` share one connection, a `disconnect()` during a connect wins; socket errors of
  mqtt5_client stay in the transport's zone; keep-alive sent in CONNECT (default 60 s); backoff
  reset only after a lasting connection; unsubscribe waits for the broker (wrong-password retry on
  one transport); sync fails fast; member writes beyond `maxItemRev` refused before publishing;
  `create` cleans up on failure. 7th round: no guarded zone around the MQTT connect (an error
  could not cross the zone boundary: `connect()` could hang, zones nested per reconnect); one
  connection attempt per try (`maxConnectionAttempts: 1`), a `disconnect()` during a connect
  lets the client disconnect once its connect settled; a failed reconnect keeps retrying;
  `deleteChannel` recovers whatever step fails; reconnects seen while deleting are kept; the
  event stream is closed without waiting (no deadlock from `await for`); a shared subscription
  wait fails with the first one. 8th round (blocking-only): a client given up on (timed out,
  refused, aborted) is dropped — socket closed, keep-alive stopped — so a late CONNACK cannot
  bring it to life (test with a fake broker); the abort handle is cleared by identity; only the
  `deleteChannel` call that set `_closing` resets it.

Still open (documented in DESIGN.md):
- Two devices of one owner changing one run at the same moment lose one change (same one
  everywhere).
- A former assignee's edit sent at the very moment of a reassignment replaces the broker copy
  until the next revision.
- A replayed old access list can be the one an owner session joins on (then builds on it).
- No tests yet for a half-open connection or a broker-initiated disconnect (transport).
- mqtt5_client may raise an uncaught SocketException when the broker resets the connection
  (apps run in an error-handling zone; not caught inside the transport).
- One session per channel per transport (a second one on the same transport gets no retained
  messages; documented on `Mqtt5Transport.subscribe`).
- Assignments of deleted tracks stay until the owner removes them.
- No transport test for "pending publishes fail on disconnect".
